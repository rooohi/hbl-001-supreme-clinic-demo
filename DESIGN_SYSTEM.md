# Design system

## Character

Calm, premium, clinical, warm, and operationally dense. The supplied Twacha logo remains unchanged. Brand red is reserved for the logo and selective brand moments; clinical errors use a distinct darker red treatment.

## Foundation

- Soft healthcare blue: `#2F6F8F`
- Muted teal: `#3C8C88`
- Twacha accent: `#E7353B`
- Warm white and light gray surfaces
- Deep slate/navy text
- Inter/Geist/system sans fallback
- 8px spacing rhythm, restrained radii, subtle borders and shadows
- 150–250 ms motion with reduced-motion compatibility

## Interaction

- Buttons use verbs and include visible disabled/configuration states.
- Focus rings remain visible; semantic form labels and `role="alert"` carry errors.
- Touch targets remain practical on mobile.
- Loading skeletons, empty states, retry states, conflict messages, and success notices are first-class.
- Lucide icons support labels; icons never replace essential copy.

## Layout

- Staff: compact left navigation, contextual top bar, high-density panels, responsive worklists.
- Patient: mobile-first linear booking with the logo, clinic status, explicit steps, consent, and clear confirmation.
- No neon, “AI” gradients, pervasive glassmorphism, cartoon medicine, giant hero typography, or decorative charts.

## Accessibility target

WCAG 2.2 AA is the target. A formal audit is still required for contrast, keyboard traps, modal focus containment, screen-reader announcements, zoom/reflow, and touch behavior across supported browsers.
